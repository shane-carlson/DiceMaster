Game-icons.net symbols bundled in DiceMaster
============================================

Source: https://game-icons.net
Repository: https://github.com/game-icons/icons

These filled SVG silhouettes are provided under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
(CC BY 3.0), or CC0 where an individual contributor marked their work as such.

A mention of the form below satisfies the license:

  Icons by Lorc, Delapouite and contributors. Available on https://game-icons.net

Authors of the broader Game-icons.net collection include Lorc, Delapouite,
John Colburn, Felbrigg, John Redman, Carl Olsen, Sbed, PriorBlue, Willdabeast,
Viscious Speed (CC0), Lord Berandas, Irongamer, HeavenlyDog, Lucas, Faithtoken,
Skoll, Andy Meneely, Cathelineau, Kier Heyl, Aussiesim, Sparker, Zeromancer (CC0),
and other contributors listed at https://game-icons.net/about.html

DiceMaster vendors a curated subset as path data in src/engine/symbols.ts.
